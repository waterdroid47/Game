Pixel Guard – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information is used by Pixel Guard (the Android app com.waterdroid47.pixelguard and the web version of the game), made by the independent developer waterdroid47 ("we"). We keep it short because the game collects very little.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not run our own servers, and the game sends nothing to us.

Your game progress (coins, gems, upgrades, levels, settings) is stored on your device. For in-app purchases, the order number that Google Play gives the game is also stored there, so that each purchase is delivered exactly once. If Android's own backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include this data in your backup and restore it when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone; that backup belongs to your Google account and we never see it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob. You choose to watch rewarded ads, for example to revive or to double your coins. A short, skippable ad can also appear between some levels. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which can give an approximate location;
  • information about your device and the app, and how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the main menu. On any Android device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Buying anything in the in-game store turns off the ads between levels on that phone. Rewarded ads stay available, because you choose when to watch them. After reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, Google Play restores the Starter Pack (and with it the ad removal); gem and coin packs are used up when bought, so only Android's backup (if turned on) can bring them back with the rest of your save.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google Play handles them under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. Refunds are requested through Google Play.

4. Web version

The browser version of the game shows no real ads and takes no real payments. It is hosted by Netlify, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation of the website. Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage.

5. Children

Pixel Guard is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your progress at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its data, or with the RESET button in the main menu. RESET keeps your one-time packs (such as the Starter Pack), the removal of ads between levels and the order numbers of past purchases. A copy in your Google account backup, if Android backup is turned on, is managed in your phone's backup settings. For data collected by Google for advertising, use the advertising ID settings described above or My Ad Center.

7. Security

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play is encrypted in transit.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact
